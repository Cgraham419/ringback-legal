RingBack SMS Program — How Consent Works

Last updated: July 2026

RingBack is an automated text-messaging service used by small service businesses (such as HVAC, plumbing, and electrical companies) to follow up with customers whose calls were not answered. This page describes exactly how customers are informed of, and consent to, this text messaging program.

Step 1 — Caller hears a disclosure

When a customer calls a business using RingBack, the following recorded message plays before the call connects:

"Thank you for calling [Business Name]. If we're unable to answer, you'll receive a one-time text asking if you'd like a follow-up by text. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP anytime to opt out."

Step 2 — If the call goes unanswered, a text is sent

The customer receives the following text message, which asks for their explicit consent before any further messages are sent:

"[Business Name]: Sorry we missed your call! Reply YES if you'd like a text follow-up about your inquiry. Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Step 3 — Consent is explicit

Only customers who reply YES to that message are enrolled to receive further texts related to their original inquiry (such as scheduling or appointment confirmations). No further messages are sent to anyone who does not reply YES.

Opting Out

Any recipient can reply STOP at any time to immediately stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Use

Mobile phone numbers collected through this program are never shared or sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details.

RingBack SMS Program — How Consent Works

Last updated: August 2026

RingBack is an automated text-messaging service used by small service businesses (such as HVAC, plumbing, and electrical companies) to follow up with customers whose calls were not answered. This page describes exactly how customers are informed of, and consent to, this text messaging program.

Step 1 — Customer calls the business

A customer calls a business's published phone number (the same number they'd find on that business's website, Google listing, or other public directory). No text message is ever sent to a number that has not first called the business directly.

Step 2 — If the call goes unanswered, a text is sent

If the business is unable to answer, the customer automatically receives the following one-time text message:

"Hi, sorry we missed your call — this is [Business Name]. What can we help with? Msg & data rates may apply. Msg frequency varies. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out."

Step 3 — Ongoing conversation

Customers who reply to this message may continue the conversation by text with the business regarding their original inquiry, including scheduling or follow-up questions. No unrelated marketing messages are sent through this program.

Opting Out

Any recipient can reply STOP at any time to immediately stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Use

Mobile phone numbers collected through this program are never shared or sold to third parties. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details.

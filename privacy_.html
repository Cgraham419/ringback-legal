Privacy Policy — RingBack

Last updated: July 2026

RingBack ("we," "us," or "our") provides an automated text-messaging service that helps small businesses respond to missed phone calls. This policy explains how we handle information collected through that service.

Information We Collect

When someone calls a business using RingBack and the call is not answered, we collect the caller's phone number and the content of any text messages exchanged with the business through our system.

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to facilitate text communication between the caller and the business they contacted, and to allow the business to track and respond to missed-call leads.

Mobile Number Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. Phone numbers collected through this service are used exclusively to deliver the automated missed-call text response and any related conversation with the business the customer contacted.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on your interaction with the business. Typically, you will receive one automated message after a missed call, plus any replies directly related to your inquiry. We do not send recurring marketing messages.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply. Contact your mobile carrier for details about your messaging plan.

Opting Out

Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data Retention

We retain message data only as long as necessary to provide the service and support the business's ability to follow up on customer inquiries.
